### Personality & Backstory

Born in Absalom to Thuvian parents — a barrister father and a carpenter mother — Ekundayo preferred manual labor and archery, taking up woodworking. After both parents died of illness during his teenage years, he became a bounty hunter in the River Kingdoms alongside a woman named **Ntavi**, whose family had been slain by giants. He eventually fell in love with **Amanda** of the village of Bristlehill, settled as a carpenter, and had a daughter, **Clarissa**.

While Ekundayo was out hunting, a band of giants led by a wyvern-hide-wearing hill giant named **Gragglebeard** slaughtered everyone in Bristlehill, including his wife and daughter. Since that day he hunts Gragglebeard and all giants with grim, stoic resolve, accompanied by an anxious dog he rescued. Those who earn his respect may call him **"Ekun."**
