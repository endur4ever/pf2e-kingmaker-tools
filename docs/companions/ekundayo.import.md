# Kingmaker Companions — Roster Import

Import this file via the kingdom sheet menu → **Import from Obsidian** to add these companions to the kingdom **companion roster** (de-duplicated by name, so re-importing is safe). After import, open each **Companion Profile** to set Influence/Discovery and add personal quests. Their full drag-and-drop actors live in the **Companions** compendium (`kingmaker-tools-companions`); full stats are in this folder's notes.

- Ekundayo (companion): Stoic Human Ranger 1 giant-hunter (Lawful Good, "Ekun" to those he trusts). Hunts the hill giant Gragglebeard, who slew his wife & daughter at Bristlehill. Travels with an anxious dog (a Riding Dog until he gains the Animal Companion feat at level 2). INFLUENCE (max 8): 2 = Indifferent (fights at your side); 4 = Friendly (shares giant-hunting methods); 8 = Helpful (−2 Construction DC on Lumber-cost structures, gifts a hooked rune). DISCOVERY DCs — Hunting Lore 13 / Perception 15 / Nature 17. WEAKNESS: promising to hunt giants/Gragglebeard = +2. PENALTY: pity/promises to help him = +2 DC until Friendly.
- Jubilost Narthropple (companion): Fey-touched Gnome Alchemist 1 (Bomber), arrogant cartographer-scholar from Taldor. INFLUENCE (max 8): 2 = Friendly (fights at your side); 4 = PCs pick his daily infused items + unlock his unique items; 8 = Helpful, grants a settlement a free alchemy laboratory + gifts alchemical items. DISCOVERY DCs — Academia Lore 13 / Crafting 15 / Perception 17. WEAKNESS: a gnome or alchemist +1 (gnome alchemist +2). PENALTY: seeming slow-witted / crit-failing an Int-skill check = −2.
- Nok-Nok (companion): Irongut Goblin Rogue 1 (Scoundrel), would-be fifth goblin hero-god, latches onto the PCs as heroes. INFLUENCE (max 12): 2 = heroic idea takes root; 4 = Friendly, follows combat orders; 6 = shifts to Chaotic Neutral; 8 = Helpful, can serve as kingdom Emissary; 12 = Chaotic Good, reveals a buried +1 shifting dogslicer. DISCOVERY DCs — Religion 18 / Perception 20 / Goblin Lore 22. WEAKNESS: witnessing chaotic mayhem = +2. PENALTY: witnessing good/charity while evil = −2.
- Tristian (companion): Aasimar Cleric of Sarenrae 1 (Cloistered healer, Nomad), unaware of his own celestial heritage. INFLUENCE (max 8): 2 = Friendly; 4 = PCs help pick his prepared spells; 8 = Helpful, grants two free settlement structures (an orphanage and a shrine) + gifts healing potions/scrolls. DISCOVERY DCs — Sarenrae Lore 13 / Religion 15 / Perception 17. WEAKNESS: a believed-orphan PC +2 (lying loses it AND 3 Influence). PENALTY: witnessing cruelty/mean jokes = −2.
