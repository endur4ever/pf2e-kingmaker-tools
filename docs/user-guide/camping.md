# Camping Subsystem Guide

The Camping subsystem automates the management of adventuring parties during downtime, handling logistics like rations and food, tracking favorite meals for progression, managing encounter preparation via the Encounter Curator, and processing rumors from rival bands.

## Logistics & Food Management

### Rations
Tracking provisions is essential to avoid starvation or forced marches. 
- **Rations Paid For Day:** The GM (or automated system) tracks how many rations are provided for the current day/night cycle.
- **Consuming Rations:** Using the "Consume Rations" button spends the available pool. If not properly managed, players may face hunger ticks or be forced into suboptimal travel modes.

### Cooking & Favorite Meals
The cooking mechanic allows parties to improve morale and track meal preferences.
- **Favorite Meal Progression:** As characters eat specific meals, they develop a preference for them. This progression is tracked via `favoriteMealProgress`.
- **Pinned Favorites:** A favorite meal can be "pinned" by the GM or player to prevent the automatic progression from changing it, ensuring a character's preferred meal remains consistent.
- **NPC Cooks:** If an NPC is assigned to cook, they can be credited with preparing meals for the party.

## Encounter Curator

The Encounter Curator allows GMs to prepare and stage encounters before they occur in play.

- **Encounter Staging:** A GM can take a rolled combat encounter (from a table result) and "Stage" it. This places tokens at a specified distance from the party, sets initiative, and prepares the scene.
- **Proxy Tables/Categories:** GMs can configure category proxy tables. When active, the system uses these weighted lists to determine which types of encounters are likely to occur in a specific region or context.
- **Rumor Board:** The Rumor Board tracks rumors generated by rival adventuring bands (Charter Parties). 
    - **Rumor Lifecycle:** A rumor is created when a rival band selects a new objective. It can be heard by the party and may eventually lead to an encounter, quest, or scene note.
    - **GM Control:** The GM manages which rumors are presented to players via whispered digests.

## Travel & Exploration Modes

The camping sheet tracks various modes of movement through the hex map:

- **Travel Mode:** Determines the pace and risks associated with moving between hexes.
- **Forced March:** A high-risk, high-speed movement option that may incur exhaustion or other penalties.
- **Region/Hex Context:** Certain settings (like region-specific encounter tables) are tied to the current hex or region where the camping sheet is active.

## Permissions & Roles

- **GM Controls:** The GM manages critical state such as travel mode, forced march status, and regional encounter overrides. These settings are intentionally excluded from player-submitted updates to prevent players from bypassing GM-controlled movement risks.
- **Player Interactions:** Players can interact with the cooking menu, select meals, and view rumors that have been officially "whispered" to them by the GM.
